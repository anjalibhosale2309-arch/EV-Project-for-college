MINI ELECTRIC BICYCLE

Simple  ·  Economical  ·  Eco-Friendly

A student-built mini electric bicycle powered by a 12V battery and 240 RPM DC motor. The motor drives the rear wheel via a chain and sprocket mechanism, achieving speeds of 5–7 kmph with a simple ON/OFF switch.

Rear Wheel Movement
When the switch is turned ON, the 12V battery supplies electrical energy to the DC motor. The motor rotates and drives the small sprocket attached to it. The chain transfers this motion to the large sprocket on the rear wheel, causing the bicycle to move forward.
