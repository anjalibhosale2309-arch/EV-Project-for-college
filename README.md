## 📌 Project Overview

The **Mini Electric Bicycle** is a student-built electric vehicle that demonstrates the fundamental working principles of EV technology using basic, low-cost components. The bicycle is powered by a **12V battery** and a **240 RPM DC motor**, which drives the rear wheel through a chain and sprocket mechanism. Speed is controlled via a simple ON/OFF switch, and the bike achieves a top speed of **5–7 km/h**.

This project was developed as part of a **Creative Technologies** course and serves as a hands-on introduction to electric vehicle design, power transmission, and sustainable engineering.

## ⚙️ How It Works

```
12V Battery → Switch (ON/OFF) → DC Motor (240 RPM) → Driving Sprocket → Chain Drive → Rear Wheel
```

When the switch is turned **ON**, the 12V battery supplies electrical energy to the DC motor. The motor rotates and drives the small sprocket attached to it. The chain transfers this motion to the large sprocket on the rear wheel, causing the bicycle to move forward.
